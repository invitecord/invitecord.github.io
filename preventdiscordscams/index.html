Verification Warning

Stop the Scam.
Secure your Account.

Phishing attempts are becoming more sophisticated. InviteCord is dedicated to mapping safe paths, but your awareness is the final layer of defense.

🎁

Fake Nitro Gifts

Attackers send DMs with links like dlscord-glft.com or discord-app.net. These are clones designed to steal your login token.

Protocol Check

Official gifts ONLY use discord.gift.

🎮

Malicious Files

"Test my game" or "New shader" scams send .exe or .src files that bypass 2FA by stealing your browser cookies.

Protocol Check

Never run unknown software from Discord.

🛡️

Staff Impersonation

Admins or Discord Staff will never DM you asking to "verify" via a QR code or by asking for your password.

Protocol Check

Staff will never ask for credentials.

Your Defense
Checklist

  • ✓
    Enable Authenticator 2FA

    Use apps like Authy or Google Authenticator. Avoid SMS 2FA.

  • ✓
    Scrutinize Every Link

    Look for dlscord, dicsord, or unusual TLDs (.net, .xyz, .gift-app).

  • ✓
    Check Authorized Apps

    Regularly audit your settings for apps with "Join Servers for you" permissions.

Emergency Recovery

If you believe your account is compromised:

01 Change your Discord password immediately. This resets your session token.
02 Refresh your 2FA backup codes.
03 Scan your PC for malware using a reputable tool.
